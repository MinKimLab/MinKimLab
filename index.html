Research Interests

Research Theme #1

Nanocomposite Materials Design & Synthesis

Synthesis of multi-dimensional organic-inorganic hybrid perovskite nanocrystals, control of organic/polymer semiconductor nanostructures and nanomorphology, and AI- and computational-chemistry-driven development of novel semiconductor nanomaterials.

Research Theme #2

Optoelectronic Semiconductor Devices

High-efficiency solar cells, luminescent nanomaterials for future displays, highly sensitive IoT smart sensors (chemical/gas/temperature/humidity), photodetectors for high-resolution image sensing, and infrared photodetectors for autonomous driving.

Research Theme #3

Next-Generation Solar Cells & Optoelectronics

Solution processes for large-area solar cells, semi-transparent solar cells for BIPV, novel flexible device architectures, and textile/attachable electronics for body-mimicking sensing devices.
