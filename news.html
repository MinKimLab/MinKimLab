    Grant

    Selected for a Samsung industry-academia collaboration project — a joint project between Prof. Kihyun Kim's group (Electronic Engineering) and Prof. Min Kim's group (Chemical Engineering): "Development of Next-Generation Perovskite-Silicon Hybrid Semiconductor Devices."

    The team will develop device technologies essential for future autonomous-mobility driving, using CMOS-compatible processes and silicon-perovskite materials. By developing domestic core technology for autonomous-driving sensors, the project is expected to strengthen Korea's competitiveness in future mobility and sensor technologies.
